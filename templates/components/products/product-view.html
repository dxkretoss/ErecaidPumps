Its code: $('.form-field--stock', $scope).addClass('u-hiddenVisually') $('[data-product-stock]', $scope).text(data.stock) adds `u-hiddenVisually` to `.form-field--stock` whenever `typeof data.stock !== 'number'` (e.g., the selected variant does not track inventory).
